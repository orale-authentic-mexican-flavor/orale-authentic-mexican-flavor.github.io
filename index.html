🇲🇽 AboutAcerca de

EN: Órale – Authentic Mexican Flavor is a food business dedicated to bringing the authentic taste of traditional homemade Mexican cuisine to Ireland. We offer traditional Mexican dishes as they are consumed in Mexico, with deep flavors, adjustable spice levels, and clearly marked vegan options.
ES: Órale – Authentic Mexican Flavor es un negocio gastronómico dedicado a traer a Irlanda el sabor auténtico de la cocina mexicana casera y tradicional. Ofrecemos platillos mexicanos tal y como se consumen en México, con sabores profundos, niveles de picante ajustables y opciones veganas claramente señalizadas.

Refund PolicyPolítica de Reembolso
Because our food is freshly prepared and perishable, orders cannot be returned once they have been handed over. If your order is incorrect, incomplete, or affected by a quality issue, please contact us within 24 hours of purchase and we will review your case. Where appropriate, we will offer a replacement or a refund at our discretion. This is a short summary — the full content of the Refund Policy is contained in the PDF below.
Debido a que nuestros alimentos se preparan al momento y son perecederos, los pedidos no pueden devolverse una vez entregados. Si tu pedido es incorrecto, está incompleto o presenta un problema de calidad, contáctanos dentro de las 24 horas posteriores a la compra y revisaremos tu caso.
