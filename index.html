Allergens & food safety

All 14 statutory allergens are managed in line with Food Safety Authority of Ireland (FSAI) requirements. The allergen tags on each dish are indicative — recipes and suppliers can change, and cross-contamination is possible in our kitchen. Always tell our staff about any allergy before you order.

Refunds

Refund Policy

Because our food is freshly prepared and perishable, orders cannot be returned once they have been handed over. If your order is incorrect, incomplete, or affected by a quality issue, please contact us within 24 hours of purchase and we will review your case.
